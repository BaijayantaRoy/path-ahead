# Code of Conduct

## The short version

This project explains the rules of some of the most stressful days in a
family's year. People who use it, and people who help build it, deserve the
same care in this community that the app tries to bring to a worried parent
at midnight: patient, plain-spoken, and never condescending.

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Disagree about a figure, a design choice,
or a pull request without making it personal. That covers almost everything.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences — especially
  disagreements about a cited figure, where the fix is a link to a better
  source, not a tone
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the families who will actually use this tool
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in any form
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion about a child's education
  into something else
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when an individual is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening an issue
labelled `conduct`, or by contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed
necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project maintainer is
obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an
incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions, as determined by the project's
other leadership.
