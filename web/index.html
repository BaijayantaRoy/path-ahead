Step one

Where is your child right now?

This one answer decides which rulebook applies. One school year apart can mean two entirely different systems — so PathAhead reads your answer back to you.

Step two

The grades

Start typing a subject — it will find it. No name, no school, no email: those fields do not exist here.

A few courses are taught in one of these and require it to apply. Answering stops PathAhead putting those in front of you if they are not yours to take — it changes nothing else, and no score goes up or down.

Step three — every question optional

A little about you

Skip all of it and PathAhead still works. Answer some and it can explain why a course might suit you — with the reasoning shown, line by line.

Not the ones you score well in — the ones you would happily do more of.

PathAhead used to decide this for you: what you enjoy counted two and a half times more than who you work with, for no reason anyone could defend. Now you decide. Set each one to how much it should count. Ties are fine, and anything set to “Doesn’t matter” is left out of the arithmetic entirely.

    This narrows the list to those fields. It does not change any score, and you can clear it at any time.
